# Admission referral partner workflows

The admission referral feature is an extension of the existing Spring Boot and MySQL application. Hibernate's existing `ddl-auto=update` setting creates the three `admission_partner`, `referral_lead` and `referral_commission` tables on startup. Existing tables and records are not dropped.

## Partner onboarding

1. A prospective partner submits `POST /api/admission-partners/register` with name, email, experience, phone (optional), and consent.
2. An administrator reviews applications at `/admin/admission-partners`.
3. Approval requires an existing StepInStudents account whose email matches the application. The user can first register through the normal student registration flow. Approval changes the user's role to `DIRECT_PARTNER` and creates a unique referral code.
4. The approved user signs in normally and opens `/admission-partners/dashboard`.
5. A direct partner can invite one subpartner level. Subpartner access also requires administrator approval and a matching existing account. Subpartners cannot invite further levels.

## Student referrals

Partners submit student details through their dashboard after obtaining explicit consent. A unique attribution identifier is recorded. The same student email cannot be submitted twice for the same partner. A partner can also share `/admission-partners?ref=CODE`; the public form validates the active code and records the referral with explicit student consent.

Administrators move a referral from `REFERRED` to `IN_REVIEW`, then to `ADMISSION_CONFIRMED` or `WITHDRAWN`. These are platform tracking states; institutions make admission decisions.

## Commission ledger

An administrator records commission only for a confirmed admission. The institutional amount and partner percentages are snapshotted as decimal values. A direct-partner share for a subpartner referral is permitted only when the administrator enabled subpartners under a verified agreement. The combined shares cannot exceed 100%; the platform share is the remainder. A caller-provided idempotency key prevents duplicate ledger postings. New entries are `PENDING_REMITTANCE`; only an administrator with a verified institutional remittance reference can mark the shares `PAYABLE`. Reversals are final and remain in the ledger. Workflow actions are recorded in `referral_audit_log`.

No earnings, agreements, admissions, or remittances are generated by the system automatically. Configure written agreements and verify institutional payment evidence before recording or reconciling commission.

## Main API routes

- Public: `POST /api/admission-partners/register`, `GET /api/admission-partners/referrals/validate?code=...`, `POST /api/admission-partners/referrals?code=...`
- Approved partner: `GET /api/admission-partners/me`, `POST /api/admission-partners/me/subpartners`
- Admin: `GET /api/admin/admission-partners`, `PATCH /api/admin/admission-partners/{id}`, `GET /api/admin/admission-partners/leads`, `PATCH /api/admin/admission-partners/leads/{id}/admission`, `GET /api/admin/admission-partners/audit`
- Finance admin: `POST /api/admin/admission-partners/commissions`, `GET /api/admin/admission-partners/commissions`, `PATCH /api/admin/admission-partners/commissions/{id}`

All request bodies use validated DTOs. Admin routes require the existing `ADMIN` role; partner workspace routes require `DIRECT_PARTNER` or `SUBPARTNER` and resolve records through the authenticated user's email.
